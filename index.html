CASE 03 · LOGISTICS · TRACKER
MariClaire Water Delivery — Tubigan Tracker

Problem: A water refilling station tracks delivery trips, counter sales, and driver cash on paper — the owner can't see today's numbers at a glance.
Solution: Flask + SQLite tracker with owner, dispatcher, and driver roles — delivery trips, customer balances, inventory, settlements, and daily reports.
Impact: Multi-role operations for a real water station — each driver sees only their trips, the owner sees every peso.

Python Flask SQLite
Demo Owner · dispatcher · driver roles
Botsi's Meatshop — butcher's ledger Demo
CASE 04 · MEATSHOP · LEDGER
Botsi's Meatshop — Butcher's Ledger

Problem: Meatshops buy live animals and cut them into retail portions — without tracking, the true cost per kilo and daily profit are guesswork.
Solution: Butcher's ledger that follows each animal from purchase (ihaw) to dressed cuts, with stock movements, cashier accounts, and per-sale profit reports.
Impact: Every animal's real cost per kilo and every sale's profit, computed automatically — staff see sales only, owners see costs.

JavaScript Role-based Reports
